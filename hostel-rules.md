# Nimbus University Hostel Rules

Fictional mock rules for this assignment.

1. Hostel entry closes at 10:00 PM.
2. Students must carry university identity cards.
3. Visitors must register at reception.
4. Students must maintain cleanliness in common areas.
5. Electrical appliances require administration approval.
6. Damaging hostel property is prohibited.
7. Follow hostel warden instructions.
8. Submit complaints to the hostel office.
